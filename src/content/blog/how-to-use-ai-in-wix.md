## Step 1: Set Up Your Site with the Wix AI Website Builder

The journey begins with the initial setup. Wix has replaced the traditional signup questionnaire with an interactive, chat-based AI website builder.

### How to initiate the AI Builder:
1. Log into your **Wix Dashboard** and click on **Create New Site**.
2. Select the option to build with **Wix AI**.
3. A conversational chat window will open. The AI will ask you questions such as:
   * *What is the name of your business or project?*
   * *What is your website's main purpose (e.g., portfolio, e-commerce store, blog, local business)?*
   * *What specific features do you need (e.g., booking systems, event RSVP, online store, contact forms)?*
   * *What vibe or style defines your brand (e.g., minimalist, bold, professional, warm)?*

Provide as much detail as possible in your chat responses. The more context you feed the AI, the more accurate and impressive your initial site draft will be. Once you complete the conversation, click **Generate Site**. Wix AI will analyze your inputs and generate a fully structured website with custom pages, placeholder text, and relevant images.

## Step 2: Customize Your Layout Using AI Section Creators

Once the AI generates your initial website, you will find yourself in the Wix Editor. If you want to add new pages or reorganize existing layouts, you don’t have to drag and drop individual elements manually. You can leverage the **AI Section Creator**.

![wix design](../../assets/how-to-use-ai-in-wix-1.jpg)

### Adding Custom Sections with AI:
* Hover over any area of your webpage and click **Add Section**.
* Select the **AI Section Creator** tool.
* Type a simple prompt describing what you want. For example: *"Add a three-column testimonial section with a soft blue background and star ratings."*
* The AI will instantly generate three unique layout variations. 
* Preview the variations, select your favorite, and click apply. The section will automatically inherit your site's global fonts and color scheme.

## Step 3: Write Captivating Copy with the Wix AI Text Generator

Writing compelling copy is often the most time-consuming part of launching a website. Wix solves this bottleneck with its integrated **AI Text Generator**, built on advanced natural language processing.

### Creating Copy for Headlines and Paragraphs:
1. Click on any text box on your page and select **Create AI Text**.
2. Input your business type, the topic of the text, and choose a tone of voice (e.g., *Professional, Casual, Playful, Persuasive*).
3. Click **Create Text**. The generator will provide three distinct copy options.
4. Select the one that fits best, or refine your prompt to try again.

### Generating Product Descriptions & Blog Outlines:
If you are running an online store, you can use the AI Text Generator inside your Wix Store Dashboard to write unique product descriptions. Simply type in the product name and key features, and the AI will generate an engaging, benefit-driven product description in seconds. For bloggers, the tool can generate SEO-friendly article outlines, catchy titles, and introductory paragraphs.

## Step 4: Create Stunning Visuals with the Wix AI Image Generator

A website is only as good as its visuals. If you do not have high-quality original photography, Wix offers an built-in **AI Image Generator** powered by cutting-edge text-to-image technology.

### How to use the AI Image Creator:
* Click on any image placeholder on your website and select **Change Image**.
* Choose the **AI Image Generator** option.
* Describe the image you need in detail. (e.g., *"A minimalist office desk with a laptop, a cup of coffee, and a monstera plant, warm morning light, photorealistic"*).
* Choose an artistic style, such as *Photo, 3D Render, Illustration, or Watercolor*.
* Click **Generate** and choose from the set of unique, royalty-free images to place on your site.

You can also use the **AI Image Eraser and Enhancer** tools to remove unwanted objects from your existing uploads or upscale low-resolution images to look crisp on high-definition screens.

## Best Practices for Using AI in Wix

While Wix's AI suite is incredibly powerful, it should be treated as a collaborative assistant rather than a completely hands-off replacement. Keep these best practices in mind to get the best results:

1. **Maintain Your Brand Voice:** AI-generated text is a fantastic starting point, but always read through it to add your unique brand personality, personal anecdotes, and internal jargon.
2. **Review for Quality and Accuracy:** Verify that any factual claims, product details, or contact information generated by AI are 100% correct.
3. **Keep Mobile Optimization in Mind:** While Wix AI designs responsive layouts, always toggle to the mobile view in the editor to ensure your buttons, texts, and images scale perfectly on smartphones.
